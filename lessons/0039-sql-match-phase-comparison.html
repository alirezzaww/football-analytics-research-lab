Football analyst training · Lesson 57

First half or second half?

Question: in our five France matches, did a higher percentage of corners lead to shots before or after half-time?

One new idea: CASE means “if”

CASE WHEN period = 1 THEN 'First half'
     WHEN period = 2 THEN 'Second half'
     ELSE 'Extra time'
END

Give each corner a label. Then use GROUP BY to count corners with the same label. Our table permits only periods 1–4; periods 3 and 4 therefore enter the extra-time group.

Use the period field. A corner at 46 minutes in period 1 happened in first-half added time.

The completed assignment

  • First half: 10 of 24 corners led to a shot → 41.7%; 12 shots.
  • Second half: 8 of 18 → 44.4%; 8 shots.
  • Extra time: 1 of 3 → 33.3%; 1 shot.

The second-half percentage is a little higher; the first half produced more shots. The 2.8 percentage-point gap does not prove that France improved after half-time. These are small, pooled descriptive groups.

The three extra-time corners all come from the Brazil match. Keep them separate. These percentages are per corner, not per minute.

How the SQL works

Summarize shots per corner → join to every corner → label its match phase with CASE → GROUP BY the label → calculate counts and rates. No delivery is lost or counted twice.

Practice: period 1, minute 46. Which group?

First half. Minute 46 here is first-half added time. The period field identifies the half.
