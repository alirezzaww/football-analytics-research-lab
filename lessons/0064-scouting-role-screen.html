Turn a requirement into a query.

Data: FIFA Women's World Cup, 2019 — StatsBomb competition 72, season 30.
Course: Soccermatics Lesson 3 — Case study: spreadsheet scouting.
SQL reference: SQLite expressions → AND and parameters. Our screen is a custom worked extension.

We can query the player pool. Now we write an illustrative football requirement and find profiles that meet it.

Our teaching requirement

Right-sided qualifying starts only
At least 3 qualifying starts
Completed eligible passes per 90 ≥30
AND
Fixed-rule completed forward passes per 90 ≥3

The two activity floors are teaching choices, recorded before this query. They are not standards for a good player or requirements supplied by a real club. The fixed forward rule means at least 20 x-coordinate units gained by a completed eligible pass.

The SQL idea

completed_per90 >= :min_completed_per90
AND forward_per90 >= :min_forward_per90
AND means both conditions must be true.

The completed assignment

We start with the 14 right-sided profiles in the original cohort. Six meet the passing floor; adding the forward condition leaves four:

  • Lucy Bronze: 45.57 completed passes/90; 4.43 fixed-rule forward passes/90.
  • Desiree van Lunteren: 33.44; 4.23.
  • Ashley Lawrence: 32.72; 3.09.
  • Risa Shimizu: 39.48; 3.06.

These are profiles to investigate under our chosen rules, not four recommended signings. Display order follows forward activity; no overall player score has been created.

Why the other rows stay visible

The full report retains every eligible profile and each condition's flag, so we can explain an exclusion. The individual forward-floor total is eight; it overlaps the passing-floor total. We do not add those two totals as separate groups.

Practice: a player has 35 completions/90 and 2.5 fixed-rule forward passes/90. Does she pass this screen?

No. She meets the passing floor but misses the forward floor. AND requires both. This means she misses this illustrative profile, not that she is a poor player.

The code compares full-precision rates. A value below 3 that displays as 3.00 still misses the forward floor. The two passing measures are related because qualifying forward passes are a subset of completed passes. Neither floor measures defensive ability or action value.
