The completed assignment

The new database adds 145 player–match activity rows across all 35 profiles. Each row contains matched registered time, completed eligible passes, fixed-rule forward completions and recorded pressures. Every player's totals reconcile to the original database.

Bronze's pooled forward-pass rate: 4.43 per 90.
Her seven included match rates: 1.89–6.67 per 90.

The overall rate does not mean she recorded 4.43 in every match. The match view shows where the observed values differ and gives us specific games to investigate. The range is descriptive, not a confidence interval or a prediction.

Use the correct denominator

Pooled rate = total actions ÷ total minutes ×90

Do not automatically average the match rates. That gives a 45-minute appearance the same weight as a 90-minute appearance.

Worked example: 3 actions in 90 minutes, then 3 in 45 minutes. What is the pooled rate?

6 actions ÷135 minutes ×90 =4 per 90. The match rates are 3 and 6, whose simple average is 4.5. The two calculations give different weights to the matches.

What the SQL joins

fullbacks:      one row per player/team profile
match_activity: one row per player/team/included match

The query joins each match row to its one parent profile using the complete IDs. We assemble event counts at player–match level first. Joining raw pass and pressure tables directly could multiply rows and inflate counts.
