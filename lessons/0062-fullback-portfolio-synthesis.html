Turn numbers into a useful report.

Read: Soccermatics Lesson 3 — Statistical scouting → Modesty. Our synthesis is a custom communication assignment.

An analyst's job includes helping other people understand what the evidence supports. This lesson brings the passing and pressure work together instead of adding another metric.

The completed assignment

I generated a combined portfolio brief with two historical profiles, exact source links and a six-event review queue. The original calculations remain unchanged. Bronze and Majri have different starting sides and team contexts; they are familiar examples, not ranked signing candidates.

Use three parts

  1. Finding: what did we observe?
  2. Limit: what does that leave unanswered?
  3. Action: what should someone inspect next?

A worked example

Finding: Bronze recorded 13.28 pressures per 90, compared with Majri's 10.89. Relative to eligible opponent passing volume, they were close: 5.01 versus 4.89 pressures per 100 attempts.

Limit: these are activity measures. They do not establish pressing success or defensive ability, and opponent passing volume is only a rough exposure proxy.

Action: inspect the linked event examples in footage when available, then check comparable situations for teammates' support, the opponent's options and what happened next. The prepared queue is not footage already reviewed.

“Bronze recorded more pressure activity per 90” is supported.
“Bronze is the better defender, so sign her” is not established by this evidence.

Why this helps your portfolio

The brief lets a reader find the question, results, limitations, sources and next observation task without reading every Python script. It honestly labels the work as assisted. Independent explanation and reproduction are still milestones for you to demonstrate.

Practice: improve “Majri's pressure success was 3 out of 7.”

“Three of the seven unique opponent passes linked to Majri's pressures were not completed. This small linked-pass subset does not prove that she caused the outcomes or measure success across all her pressures.”

Run 80_fullback_portfolio_synthesis.py in Spyder to rebuild the report from saved evidence. No new data or model is introduced. The script checks player identities, cohort membership, fixture scope, minutes and pressure totals before combining results.

Ask your teacher about any unclear finding or denominator. A useful next independent exercise is to explain the brief's first two findings in your own words, keeping each denominator clear.
