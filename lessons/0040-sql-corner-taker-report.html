Football analyst training · Lesson 58

Build a player summary.

We combine the SQL skills into a football report: who delivered France's corners, and how many led to a shot?

The completed assignment

  • Amel Majri: 10/24 → 41.7%.
  • Gaëtane Thiney: 8/18 → 44.4%.
  • Onema Grace Geyoro: 0/2 → 0%.
  • Elise Bussaglia: 1/1 → 100%.
A high percentage from one attempt is weak evidence about a player's ability. Always show the counts.

New SQL idea: HAVING

GROUP BY t.passer
HAVING COUNT(*) >= :min_deliveries

GROUP BY builds one result per player name. HAVING keeps only groups meeting a condition. When min_deliveries is 10, only Majri and Thiney appear. Ten is an example display filter, not a rule that makes results reliable.

WHERE filters individual input records before grouping. HAVING filters the groups afterward. Keep zero-shot corners in the denominator; do not filter them out before calculating the rate.

How we connected the data

Each corner gets one taker record, matched by match ID and corner ID. The loader verifies that every corner has exactly one such record. We summarize shots per corner before joining, then group corners by taker. Across all four players, the counts still total 45 corners and 19 with a shot.

These are name groups in our small saved sample. Larger player databases need stable player IDs. Differences here also reflect side, delivery type and match context; they are not an adjusted player ranking.

Practice: change the minimum from 10 to 20. Who remains?

Only Majri, with 24 deliveries. Thiney's 18 does not meet the filter. Their original percentages do not change when we hide a group.
