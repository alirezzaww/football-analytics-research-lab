Who acted, and who started the sequence?

Data: FIFA Women's World Cup, 2019 — StatsBomb competition 72, season 30.
Read: Soccermatics Lesson 3 — Normalisation → Pressure Regains.
Field definitions: StatsBomb specification → team, possession and possession_team. This audit is our custom extension.

The previous lesson left some pressure outcomes unclear. Now we inspect the underlying labels rather than quietly counting those cases as failures.

Two fields with different jobs

  • team: the team performing this event.
  • possession_team: the team that started this recorded possession sequence in control.
A possession-sequence label is not a frame-by-frame record of who touches the ball.

A real example

England–Scotland, 9 June 2019, first half. At 06:43.781, the data records:

Event type:       Pass
team:             Scotland W
possession:       17
possession_team:  England Women's

Scotland performed the pass. The sequence was still labelled England. These fields answer different questions; we have not shown that the provider made an error.

Bronze's pressure at 06:45.362 is also inside sequence 17, labelled England. Scotland's next sequence, number 18, starts at 06:45.418. This is event-data context, not an observation from footage.

The completed assignment

We audited all 240 pressure cases carrying their own team's possession label from lesson 77. For 200, the same labelled sequence contains at least one opponent Pass or Carry somewhere in it. Several pressures can share a sequence, so 200 is not a count of unique possessions.

All 240 pressure timestamps follow their sequence's first-index event timestamp. Our check therefore found no reversal of those two times. That does not validate every timestamp or explain every label.

Bronze's 21 and Majri's 18 unclear cases remain unchanged. We have not relabelled them or converted them into successful/failed pressures.

What you should understand

Read a field's definition before using it as a football conclusion. “The sequence started with England in control” does not mean “every event in that sequence is an England action.” An opponent touch also does not automatically establish a new controlled possession.

Practice: a Pass has team=France and possession_team=Brazil. Who made the pass?

France. Brazil is the possession-sequence label. We need more context to explain the passage of play or judge whether a recovery occurred.
