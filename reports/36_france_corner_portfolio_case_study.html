Three findings to carry forward

  • Shot-associated phases occur across all five fixtures, but both observed phase goals came against Korea Republic.
  • Delivery-type groups mix takers and sides. There are 0 same-taker/side/match groups with both recorded inswingers and outswingers; this limits direct comparison.
  • 9 of 45 technique labels are unrecorded. Missing labels stay visible instead of being guessed.

Decision supported: prioritize targeted review

Use the 6-example review queue to inspect first contact, receiving control and defensive marking. The present evidence supports review priorities; it does not establish a preferred delivery technique or target zone. Footage has not yet been reviewed.

Definition and limits

Count every recorded corner Pass event, including incomplete deliveries. Associate same-team non-penalty shots within 15 seconds in the same possession, ending at a new restart, period/possession change or goal. Periods 1–4 are included and each shot is assigned once. This is a descriptive phase proxy, not causal corner value or a measurement of intended targets.

The time-window check on the Korea Republic fixture changes from 4/13 corners at five seconds to 6/13 at 10, 15 and 20 seconds. That sensitivity check has not been repeated across all five matches. The pooled rate weights deliveries equally: sum positive corners, then divide by all deliveries.
