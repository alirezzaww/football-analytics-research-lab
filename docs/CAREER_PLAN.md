## Historical recruitment case study started

Lesson 63 defines a reproducible 35-player fullback profiling pool from all 52 2019 World Cup fixtures using a fixed three-start role filter. This extends the guided portfolio toward recruitment questions. It does not establish current player availability or independent scouting skill. Lesson 64 adds guided registered-time audits in qualifying starting-role fixtures, with an explicit added/extra-time convention; independent official minute totals and position-specific time were not validated. Lesson 65 adds initial passing activity per 90 on the same selected-fixture scope, without possession/difficulty adjustment or ability rankings. Lesson 66 adds a transparent completed-pass x-advancement definition, preserving the same sample and minutes; it is not a universal progression or action-value metric. Lesson 67 audits starting-area composition and geometric room without changing the original results; this does not fully adjust for context. Lesson 68 tests a documented near-goal alternative alongside the fixed definition, without claiming validated action-value improvement. Lesson 69 packages two familiar examples into a concise generated brief and four-event review queue. No footage has been watched and the players are not ranked as signings. Lesson 70 adds matched-window team passing shares as descriptive involvement context; this is not a fully possession-adjusted ability measure. Lesson 71 adds documented cohort-relative percentiles for activity and involvement, with explicit ties and starting-side pools; these do not establish general ability. Lesson 72 checks dependence on individual own matches with other peers fixed; the reported ranges are not confidence intervals. Lesson 73 adds recorded defensive pressure activity and location coverage in the same windows; it does not establish successful pressing or defensive quality. Next: independent explanation/reproduction, actual video observation when footage is available, and further contextual comparison.

Lesson 75 adds guided spatial pressure profiles with reconciled event keys and explicit missing-location denominators. Location counts/shares are descriptive, not evidence of independent scouting, successful pressing or tactical instructions.
