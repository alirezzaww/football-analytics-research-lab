A recruitment-analysis workflow

Competition / season: FIFA Women’s World Cup, 2019 (StatsBomb competition 72, season 30).
Current reading: Soccermatics Lesson 3 — Statistical scouting, Context: what to measure.
Current practice: Lesson 67 — starting-area context.
  1. Define the football role and question.
  2. Define the comparison population and sample filters.
  3. Audit identities, roles, appearances and playing time.
  4. Calculate relevant measures with clear denominators.
  5. Compare with positional, team and match context.
  6. Use evidence to nominate video-review questions and candidates for further assessment.

Cohort: the players who meet an explicit set of analysis rules. Eligibility is not an ability score. A role label at kick-off does not establish the role for every minute.

Identity: use player IDs rather than assume names are unique. Preserve team identity and label changes.

Exposure: starts are not minutes; per-90 normalization does not automatically adjust for possession, role or opposition. Sample filters are chosen rules, not universal reliability guarantees.

Historical data: a 2019 exercise cannot establish current player form, price, contract status or availability. A statistical pool is one stage in assessment.

First worked recruitment lesson · Primary course source: statistical scouting.

Current denominator convention: sum registered intervals in qualifying fullback-start fixtures; include recorded added/extra time, exclude halftime breaks and shootouts, retain temporary absences. Later role changes stay within these match intervals, so do not label the result as verified fullback-position minutes. Counts and minutes must cover the same fixtures and active intervals.

Passing activity versus completion percentage

Attempts per 90 = eligible attempts ÷ selected registered minutes × 90. Completions per 90 uses completed attempts as its numerator. Completion percentage = completions ÷ attempts × 100, without minutes. Unknown/injury-clearance outcomes and restart deliveries are excluded in this project. These measures describe activity and observed outcomes; possession and difficulty remain unadjusted.

Current forward-advancement definition

For completed eligible passes, end_x − start_x ≥ 20 on the StatsBomb 0–120 length scale. This project threshold is not metres, total pass length, a universal progressive-pass standard or a defender-line measure. Per-90 counts use the same selected registered minutes; shares use all eligible completions. Missing coordinates stop the analysis. Short valuable passes near goal can fail this rule.

Starting-area context

Coarse thirds: 0≤x<40, 40≤x<80, 80≤x≤120. Compare counts and shares with explicit within-group denominators. For a fixed 20-unit gain, x>100 has insufficient coordinate room; x=100 can qualify at end_x=120. Geometric room is not a verified passing option. Keep original results visible; grouping by a third does not equalize pressure, opponents or role.
