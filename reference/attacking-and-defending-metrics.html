Whose action are we measuring?

For: actions performed by the focus team. Against: actions performed by its opponents while facing the focus team.

Attacking corner-shot rate:
Focus-team corners with a focus-team shot ÷ focus-team corners taken

Defensive corner-shot rate conceded:
Opponent corners with an opponent shot ÷ opponent corners faced

Keep the fixture population and phase definition explicit. Rates for and against use different sets of deliveries, so they do not add to 100%. Pool counts before dividing. Zero exposure means an undefined rate.

Association counts do not show whether marking was good, a clearance was controlled, or a later shot fell outside the rule. Add video observations and context before making tactical claims.

Frequency, quality and outcome

Shots count attempts; mean xG estimates their average scoring chance; actual goals record outcomes. Summed xG is an expected-goal total, not the chance of at least one goal. xG per corner = shots per corner × mean xG per shot. Do not substitute corners with a shot for shot count when one corner can produce multiple attempts.

Shot freeze frames

A recorded snapshot of player positions at the shot, not continuous tracking. The teammate label refers to the shooting team. Keep goalkeepers separate when counting outfield defenders. A point inside the shot-to-goalposts triangle is geometric presence, not verified blocking ability. Recorded-player counts need not equal all players on the pitch; missing frames give undefined counts.
