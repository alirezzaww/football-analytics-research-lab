Whose action are we measuring?

For: actions performed by the focus team. Against: actions performed by its opponents while facing the focus team.

Attacking corner-shot rate:
Focus-team corners with a focus-team shot ÷ focus-team corners taken

Defensive corner-shot rate conceded:
Opponent corners with an opponent shot ÷ opponent corners faced

Keep the fixture population and phase definition explicit. Rates for and against use different sets of deliveries, so they do not add to 100%. Pool counts before dividing. Zero exposure means an undefined rate.

Association counts do not show whether marking was good, a clearance was controlled, or a later shot fell outside the rule. Add video observations and context before making tactical claims.
