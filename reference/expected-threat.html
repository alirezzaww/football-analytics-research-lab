Position-based expected threat

xG: estimates a shot's scoring probability from its situation.

xT: values a possession location under a specified continuation model.

Zone value = immediate scoring reward + weighted destination values
Completed-move added xT = destination value − starting value

Use unsuccessful movement attempts in fitting denominators; only successful transitions retain future value. Exact population/action/termination rules define the model. Location-only models omit defensive and sequence context.

A signed negative value is not a complete decision assessment. A zero within-cell change may hide useful movement. Do not rank players from a few selected completed-pass examples, and distinguish training fit from independent validation.

Outcome coverage: standard successful-move xT does not fully assess all attempts. A project scenario may assign failed passes minus starting value by assuming zero continuation, but this is an explicit assumption: a loose ball or retained restart can preserve value. Keep unknown outcomes excluded and labelled. Worked diagnostic.

Hypothetical decision value: p × success value + (1−p) × failure value − start value. Assessments of real choices require credible probabilities and available alternatives. A realized result alone does not establish decision quality. Worked probability exercise.

Completion estimates: fit group success rates on training observations only. Smoothing toward a training reference reduces extremes in sparse groups; its strength is a modelling choice. A starting-zone probability is different from both xT value and the probability of a specific proposed option. First completion model.

Match cross-validation: omit one whole fixture, refit on the others and predict only the omitted fixture; rotate and pool unique predictions. Fit baselines and preprocessing inside each fold. Grouped validation on inspected data is not a fresh or necessarily chronological test. Worked match checks.

Calibration: compare mean predicted probability with the observed completed/attempted fraction for groups of held-out predictions. This differs from knowing an individual pass's true probability. Grouping mixes contexts, and agreement in groups does not establish universal reliability. Worked probability check.
