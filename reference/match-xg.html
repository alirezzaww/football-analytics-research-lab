Read shot volume and quality together

Shots: number of included attempts.

Total xG: sum of their scoring probabilities.

xG per shot: total xG divided by shots; undefined if there are no shots.

Use matching inclusion rules and one model for comparisons. Label non-penalty totals as npxG. Count each shot once, even when several passes preceded it. Own goals and penalties can make shot-goal totals differ from the final score.

Totals may exceed 1: they are expected goal counts, not win probabilities. A higher total is not a guaranteed result. A single goals-minus-xG gap does not prove finishing ability. Summing expectations requires no independence assumption; converting the probabilities into a score distribution requires additional modelling.

Cumulative xG: running sum in shot order. A shot adds its probability whether scored or missed; a goal marker adds no extra xG. Separate periods to retain added time correctly. Flat stretches mean no included shots, not no possession.
